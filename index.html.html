Validated by duplicate-grouped cross-validation: AUC = 0.674 (138 respondents, 98 unique response patterns). Use it to rank and discuss projects, not to decide on a single contract.

Source. Al Momani, Y. Leakage-free machine learning prediction of cost overrun frequency in road projects from practitioner survey data. Data: open Kaggle survey of 143 practitioners in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah.

Reading the signs. Only C29 raises the risk. The negative coefficients of C14 and C19 do not mean that late payment or design changes reduce overrun; for the same concern about coordination, respondents who stress payment and design issues were more often in the "rarely" group.
